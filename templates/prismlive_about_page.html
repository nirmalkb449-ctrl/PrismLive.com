WHO WE ARE

A Creative Ecosystem Built for Talent

Prism Live is an initiative focused on discovering, developing and connecting creative talent across multiple disciplines.

We bring together writers, performers, technicians, creators, mentors and production professionals in one connected creative ecosystem.

Through structured programs, workshops, mentoring and real-world projects, we create pathways for talent to move from discovery to meaningful creative work.
